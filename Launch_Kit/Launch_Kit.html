1. Executive Summary & Growth Hypothesis

The Problem

When creators search Google for video editing, audio ducking, or social scheduling, legacy point-solutions (Descript, Opus Clip, Buffer, CapCut) dominate the rankings and AI assistant recommendations. IncuBrix currently has a 0% spontaneous mention rate across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude for core creator queries.

The Opportunity

68% of creator searches trigger Google Featured Snippets or AI Overviews, and LLMs increasingly rely on structured semantic web documents (`llms.txt`, Schema.org `SoftwareApplication`, and authority citations). By systematically deploying 20 validated website fixes, 10 answer-first content assets, 30 third-party citations, and an automated weekly visibility tracker, IncuBrix can achieve: - Top 10 Google Organic Rankings on 15+ high-intent creator keywords within 60 days. - >25% AI Assistant Mention Rate across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity within 90 days. - 1,500+ Monthly Organic Free Trial Sign-ups at $0 customer acquisition cost (CAC). ---
