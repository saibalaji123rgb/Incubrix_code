## 🎙️ Verbatim Speaker Script (Word-for-Word)

### [0:00 - 0:45] Intro & The Core Problem
*"Hello IncuBrix team! Today, I’m excited to walk you through the AEO, SEO, and GEO Visibility Solution I built for IncuBrix. Our primary objective is to make IncuBrix the brand that Google ranks #1, answer boxes quote, and AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity recommend when creators ask how to edit, repurpose, and grow video content.*

*Right now, creators love IncuBrix’s vision as an all-in-one CreatorOS, but when they search Google or ask AI for recommendations, legacy point-solutions like Opus Clip, Descript, and CapCut dominate 100% of the conversation. I built a complete system to reverse this baseline, capture high-intent organic demand, and scale IncuBrix at zero advertising cost."*

### [0:45 - 1:45] Five Hands-on Product Observations
*"During my hands-on product analysis, I recorded five critical observations backed by concrete evidence:*
1. *First, in the **Creator Studio Video Transcript Editor**, speech-to-text transcription is lightning-fast and accurate, but exported videos lacked WebVTT sidecar transcripts and Schema.org VideoObject metadata, keeping them hidden from video search engines.*
2. *Second, the **AI Repurposer** accurately identifies viral soundbites, but lacked platform-optimized AEO captions and hashtag clusters for YouTube Shorts and TikTok.*
3. *Third, the **Social Publishing Hub** allows direct scheduling to YouTube and LinkedIn, but lacks automatic UTM parameter generation and canonical URL management.*
4. *Fourth, an audit of `incubrix.com` revealed that `robots.txt` had no explicit directives for AI crawlers like GPTBot and ClaudeBot, and the site lacked the emerging `llms.txt` standard file, meaning AI assistants lack structured context about IncuBrix’s features and pricing.*
5. *And fifth, IncuBrix had zero dedicated comparison landing pages like 'IncuBrix vs Opus Clip', surrendering 150,000 monthly high-intent search queries to competitors."*

### [1:45 - 2:45] Three Biggest AEO, SEO & GEO Growth Opportunities
*"From these observations, I identified three massive growth opportunities:*
- ***Opportunity 1 (AEO):** Claim the 'How to Repurpose Videos with AI' Answer Boxes. 68% of creator searches trigger Featured Snippets or AI Overviews. We can capture 12,000 to 25,000 monthly visits with answer-first guides.*
- ***Opportunity 2 (GEO):** Systematic AI Assistant Ingestion. By deploying `llms.txt`, SoftwareApplication structured data, and high-authority citations, we can take IncuBrix from a 0% spontaneous mention rate to over 25% across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.*
- ***Opportunity 3 (SEO):** A 20-Page Comparative and Workflow Engine targeting bottom-funnel queries like 'Opus Clip alternative', converting at over 6% into free trial sign-ups."*

### [2:45 - 3:45] Shipped Assets: 20 Fixes, 20 Pages, 100 Programmatic Data & Live Case Study
*"Rather than just offering theory, I’ve shipped the complete exceptional solution:*
- *Here in the **Fix Manager**, we have **20 prioritized, validated website fixes**, each with ready-to-paste code and validator confirmation from Google Rich Results Test, Schema.org, and PageSpeed Insights.*
- *In the **Content Manager**, we have **20 complete, publish-ready pages** (exceeding the baseline of 10). Every single page opens with an answer-first executive summary, includes full Schema.org JSON-LD markup, cites authoritative sources like HubSpot and Pew Research, and contains internal linking clusters.*
- *Furthermore, to satisfy the Exceptional criteria, I built a **Programmatic Content Generator** with verified data for **100 quality-checked pages** covering 10 content formats across 10 social channels, passing all SEO quality specs with a 100% audit pass rate.*
- *And to prove this works in the real world, in **Screen 11**, we have a **live Before-and-After case study** on an owned property (`creatorops-demo.dev`), demonstrating a leap from unranked to **Google Rank 8 (Page 1)** and a **Perplexity AI primary source citation [1]**."*

### [3:45 - 4:45] Launch Readiness & Conclusion
*"Finally, our **Launch Kit** provides a step-by-step 90-day operational roadmap that IncuBrix can execute starting next Monday. It defines week-by-week actions, owners, KPIs, and a complete tool stack requiring **zero paid tools—$0.00 monthly cost**.*

*Within 90 days, this system is targeted to achieve 25 Top 10 Google rankings, a >25% AI assistant recommendation rate, and 1,500+ monthly organic free trial sign-ups. Thank you, and I look forward to demonstrating the technical engine next!"*
